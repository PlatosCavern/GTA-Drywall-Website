Finish carpentry and trim in Toronto and the GTA

GTA Drywall & Carpentry is a union drywall and carpentry contractor. Our finish carpentry covers casing, baseboard, crown moulding and the detail work that finishes a room, in commercial and residential projects across Toronto and the GTA.

What's included

  • Door and window casing
  • Baseboard
  • Crown moulding
  • Trim carpentry and detail work around openings, arches and ceilings
  • Trim fitted after drywall and before paint

Commercial and residential

In commercial work, trim carpentry is part of the fit-out, finishing doors, windows and room edges once the walls are up. In homes it is the visible detail of a renovation or addition: the casing around a door, the baseboard along a wall, the crown where wall meets ceiling.

Because we also board, tape and frame, the carpentry crew works with the same people who built the walls. Trim meets a surface that was finished with it in mind.

How we work

We walk the space and confirm scope before work starts, so there are no surprises partway through. We plan around occupied spaces and active sites, and keep you posted on timing as work progresses. Dust control and daily cleanup are part of the job, not an afterthought.

Trim goes on after the walls are boarded and finished, so it pairs with our drywall installation and taping and finishing.
