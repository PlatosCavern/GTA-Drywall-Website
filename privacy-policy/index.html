Privacy policy

This site does not run a contact form, analytics, cookies or tracking of any kind. Browsing this site does not send us any personal information.

What information we receive

The only personal information that reaches GTA Drywall & Carpentry is what you choose to send us directly, by phone or by email, when you get in touch about a job. That may include your name, phone number, email address and whatever details you share about the project.

How we use it

We use the information you send us to respond to your enquiry and, if the job goes ahead, to carry out and coordinate the work. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

What this site does not do

This site has no contact form, no analytics or tracking scripts, no cookies and no chat widget. We do not collect your information through your use of this website. This site does load its typefaces from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), which means Google receives your device's IP address when a page loads.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@gtadrywallandcarpentry.com or by phone at 647-449-0694.
